PROTOTYPE — BLIND BAKE-OFF

Heal bake-off: what should a same-camera cut look like?

Each card shows the same 3 seconds around one mid-shot cut, healed several different ways, labelled in a shuffled order. Cuts with someone speaking get four versions; cuts without speech get six, because two methods are only allowed where no one speaks. For each card, pick the version you'd notice least (best) and most (worst). The clips loop; unmute for sound (the audio is identical in every version of a card). When you're done, press Reveal to see which method was which, then Copy results and paste them in chat.
