Liquid Retime: carve-only seam carving in time

Question: can a film be shortened by removing only original frames and audio, with no synthesis and no speed change, before anyone notices? Every frame below is ranked by how cheap it is to cut (signal-only scores: motion, loudness, the discontinuity a cut would create, spread). Drag Target length and the carved version plays immediately: removed seams are skipped live, like the slider in liquid rescale. The preview has no crossfades and seeks can stutter, so judge the result from the real renders at the bottom.
